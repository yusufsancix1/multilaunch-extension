\# 🚀 MultiLaunch - Fast Tab Launcher



Save your frequently used websites and launch them in new tabs instantly—either one by one with a single click, or all together at once!



\## 📦 How to Install (100% Free)



1\. Click the green \*\*Code\*\* button at the top right of this page and select \*\*Download ZIP\*\*.

2\. Extract the downloaded ZIP file into a folder on your computer.

3\. Open Google Chrome and type `chrome://extensions/` in the address bar.

4\. Turn on the \*\*Developer mode\*\* toggle switch in the top right corner of the Extensions page.

5\. Click the \*\*Load unpacked\*\* button that appears in the top left corner.

6\. Select the folder where you extracted the ZIP file.



🎉 \*\*Success!\*\* MultiLaunch is now installed. Click the puzzle piece icon in the top right corner of Chrome to pin it to your toolbar and start using it.



\## 🛠️ Features

\- \*\*Local Storage:\*\* Your shortcuts are securely saved directly in your browser.

\- \*\*Dual Launch Modes:\*\* Open links individually by clicking on them, or launch the entire list simultaneously.

\- \*\*Clean \& Fast UI:\*\* Dark-mode optimized, minimalist design that loads instantly.
